# Instructions de génération des décors

Outil utilisé : génération d’images intégrée à Codex, sans appel par script API.

## assets/ecole.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen background for a French educational escape game called AURA. Create one wide 16:9 cinematic architectural photograph, eye-level first-person viewpoint, credible contemporary French higher-education training campus. Coherent architecture across all scenes: pale limestone, warm oak, glass partitions, thin black metal frames, plants, understated turquoise touchscreen terminals, warm evening interior lights and blue hour outside. Realistic architectural materials, grounded proportions, subtly tense after-class deserted atmosphere, beautiful but functional, no spaceships, no holograms, no robots, no people, no visible writing, no logos, no watermark, no borders, no UI. Full usable scene, especially clear central and left areas; keep right third relatively calm to accommodate an overlaid question card. Exterior entrance of a modern two-story training school at dusk, glass facade glowing warmly, broad pedestrian entrance, courtyard paving, small trees and plants, welcoming oak soffit and glass double doors, a small turquoise access terminal near the doors, campus visible rather than vast corporate tower.
